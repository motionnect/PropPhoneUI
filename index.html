Prop Phone

Fake phone screens for the camera. Set up a prop, then tap Start. To leave a prop, tap the top-left corner of the screen 3 times.

Prop message

One message per line. me: is what the actor sends, them: is the reply. Lines before the first me: show as chat history. When the script runs out, the actor's real typing is sent.

Prop call

Leave the ringtone empty to use the default ring.

Prop video call

The camera asks for permission once. Tap the screen to show the controls. The flip button switches front and back camera.

Prop notifications

One per line: app | title | text | seconds. Seconds count from the moment you start. Built-in styles: sms, chat, social, mail, calendar. Any other app name gets its own colour.

The prop starts with the screen off. Each notification wakes it. Old notifications stay on the lock screen when it wakes again.

The lock screen wallpaper and clock come from Home and lock screen. Tap a notification to dismiss it.

Home and lock screen

App icons

Add icon images for the apps your scene needs. Screenshot the icon on your phone, crop it square, then save it here under the app's name. Notifications and the home screen use it whenever the app name matches.
